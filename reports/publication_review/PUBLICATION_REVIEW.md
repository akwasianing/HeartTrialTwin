### Remaining risk if the password stays unchanged

Sanitizing an example, ignoring `.env`, or preparing a clean commit does not
revoke an exposed credential and does not remove any historical copies.
Someone with that password and access to the applicable service could exercise
the account's permissions. A local endpoint/read-only account limits exposure,
but does not prove the password is safe, unreused, or that access grants will
remain unchanged. We have not attempted authentication with an old credential,
audited external copies, or certified the account's current security in this
task. Rotation/revocation remains recommended; the owner has declined it for now.
Content-publication review and account-security clearance are distinct.
